# Story bible (original arcs)

Status: **skeleton**. Original final arcs (senior-team call-up, a rival's redemption/downfall, a Ballon d'Or race, a mentor's retirement) are milestone M6+; nothing below is implemented.

## Invented cast (see `src/content/characters/original.ts`)
- **Sora Kaizuka**, the teammate you passed to in the qualifying final. Redemption-seeker; engine or wound depending on your answer.
- **Reiji Hasegawa**, the academy golden boy who watched you get cut.
- **Mio Tachibana**, journalist; remembers every public contradiction.
- **Haruka Shirane**, your younger sister (hometown contact).
- **Dante Cavalli / Lukas Brandt / Tomás Ferreira**, Italian / German / Spanish stratum prodigies for the NEL.
- **Ayame Kisaragi**, sponsor executive.

## Principles for original arcs
Each arc ends with an Arc Report (what changed, who you are now, carried flags). Original antagonists must be reachable through earlier relationship state, never appear from nowhere.
